# US5: Exploratory Data Analysis - Key Findings

1. **High Risk Proportion:** The distribution of final results shows that the number of at-risk students is almost equal to successful students, confirming the critical need for an early warning system.
2. **Assessment Scores Gap:** The histogram of mean scores clearly shows that at-risk students have significantly lower average scores, while successful students score much higher.
3. **Engagement Gap:** The boxplot for weekly activity reveals that successful students have a significantly higher median number of clicks compared to at-risk students.
4. **Divergence over Time:** The activity curves demonstrate that while initial engagement might be similar, the activity of at-risk students drops significantly by the last 2 weeks, showing when the groups start to diverge.
5. **Actionable Insights:** Low assessment scores combined with declining weekly activity are strong indicators of risk. Teachers and curators can use these metrics to intervene early.
6. **Hypothesis Confirmed:** The hypothesis that low engagement correlates with a higher risk of failure or withdrawal is fully confirmed by the OULAD dataset.
